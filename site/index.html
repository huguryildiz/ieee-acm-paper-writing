Three distinct authorities.

  1. Scientific support

    What verified formulations, implementations, data, experiments, and cited literature establish.

  2. Method and domain reporting

    What the applicable engineering methods and domain conventions require authors to disclose for reproducibility and interpretation.

  3. Venue-specific compliance

    What the selected publication’s current official instructions and template require — kept separate from scientific validity.

These authorities must remain distinct: venue requirements do not establish scientific validity, and polished prose cannot compensate for missing evidence. Eight engineering profiles provide method- and domain-specific reporting checks.
