1

Simulation is presented as deployment evidence

III. Methods · opening sentence
Critical
We deploy the controller on autonomous warehouse robots and evaluate it under realistic operating conditions.

Contradicted or missing evidence

The fixture supplies simulator logs only. It contains no physical robot, sensor, actuator, warehouse, or field-deployment record.

Scientific consequence

The sentence changes simulated evidence into a real-system deployment claim.

1

Bounded response

Scientific support
Evidence-scoped rewrite

State that the controller is evaluated in a simulated warehouse environment. Do not claim hardware or operational performance without corresponding evidence.

2

System and controller contract is absent

III. Methods · controller description
Major
The controller converts localization estimates into safe motion commands.

Contradicted or missing evidence

No state, observation, action, update rule, control frequency, dynamics model, actuator bound, or definition of safe motion is supplied.

Scientific consequence

A reader cannot reconstruct the controller or determine which safety property the sentence refers to.

2

Bounded response

Method / domain reporting
External author query

Request the state and observation definitions, controller update rule, execution frequency, dynamics and actuator constraints, and the exact safety criterion. Keep the safety wording out of manuscript prose until those items and supporting evidence are available.

3

Simulator and scenario contract is absent

III. Methods · simulation description
Major
We use a high-fidelity simulator with realistic sensor noise and randomized warehouse layouts.

Contradicted or missing evidence

No archived artifact identifies the simulator and version, world files, dynamics, sensor-noise model, layout distribution, seed list, or controller revision.

Scientific consequence

High-fidelity and realistic are not reproducible properties, and the tested environment distribution cannot be reconstructed or bounded.

3

Bounded response

Method / domain reporting
External author query

Request the simulator and version, world-file identity, dynamics and sensing configuration, noise and layout distributions, seed list, and controller revision. Replace high-fidelity and realistic with a factual description limited to the supplied configuration.

4

Mean latency is treated as a real-time guarantee

IV. Evaluation · timing paragraph
Major
An average inference time of 18 ms confirms real-time operation.

Contradicted or missing evidence

The synthetic timing record provides a mean of 18 ms but no control period, deadline, tail distribution, jitter, hardware identity, warm-up policy, or missed-deadline count.

Scientific consequence

A mean computation time cannot establish deadline compliance or real-time behavior.

4

Bounded response

Scientific support
Evidence-scoped rewrite

Report only the observed mean per-step inference time of 18 ms for the fixture configuration. Treat real-time compliance as unevaluated until the deadline, hardware, timing distribution, and misses are supplied.

5

Preprocessing leaks evaluation data

III. Methods · data preparation
Major
We normalize all recorded trajectories before assigning frames to training and test sets.

Contradicted or missing evidence

The normalization statistics use the complete synthetic dataset, including test trajectories. Frames are then split after preprocessing.

Scientific consequence

Information from the evaluation trajectories enters model fitting, so the reported held-out result is not a clean estimate.

5

Bounded response

Method / domain reporting
External author query

Split by complete trajectory first, fit every preprocessing transform on the training trajectories only, apply the frozen transforms to the test trajectories, and rerun the evaluation. Do not repair this finding with wording alone.

6

Frames are counted as independent replications

IV. Evaluation · uncertainty statement
Major
Performance is stable across 6,000 independent test samples.

Contradicted or missing evidence

The 6,000 frames come from 12 simulated runs. Frames within a run share the same trajectory, environment realization, and controller state.

Scientific consequence

The stated denominator overcounts the independent replication unit and can understate uncertainty.

6

Bounded response

Scientific support
Evidence-scoped rewrite

Identify the 12 simulated runs as the replication units, report the 6,000 frames as correlated observations, and compute uncertainty at the run level or justify another dependence-aware analysis.

7

Divergent runs disappear from the denominator

IV. Evaluation · run selection
Major
We report the 12 successful runs after removing unstable trials.

Contradicted or missing evidence

The fixture contains 15 attempted runs; three were removed after controller divergence, and no prospective exclusion rule is supplied.

Scientific consequence

Conditioning the analysis on success hides a 3/15 failure fraction and biases the reported performance.

7

Bounded response

Method / domain reporting
Evidence-scoped rewrite

Report all 15 attempted runs and the three divergence failures. Analyze the complete set or apply a prospectively justified exclusion rule while preserving the failure count and its consequence.
